### Project Description
Sellease is a dynamic and user-friendly e-commerce platform designed to connect buyers and sellers seamlessly. It provides a secure and efficient marketplace where individuals and businesses can list, sell, and purchase a wide range of products across various categories.

### The Problem statement
Sellease is solving the ridiculous problem of people struggling to sell things they don’t want—but somehow keeping them forever because listing them online is too much of a hassle.We're also tackling the absurd reality of shoppers endlessly scrolling through overpriced junk, wondering if they’ll ever find a good deal. Sellease cuts through the clutter with AI-driven recommendations and real-time price comparisons.

### The Solution
"No More Time Wasters" Mode – Tired of “Is this still available?” messages? We auto-reply YES, IT IS! and even give buyers a countdown timer to commit before the next person grabs it.
